Frequently asked questions

How do I set my calorie goal?

During setup, or any time in Profile › Set Goals, tap "How many calories do I need?" to estimate a target from your age, height, weight, activity level and goal.

What is included in Premium?

Premium unlocks barcode scanning, micronutrient tracking and the full recipe library. New users get a free 2-week trial.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple account: open the app's Subscription screen and tap "Manage in App Store", or go to Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your device.

How do I delete my data?

Open Profile and tap "Delete Account" to permanently erase all of your data from the device.

Where does the food data come from?

Generic foods are provided by USDA FoodData Central, and barcode products by Open Food Facts.
